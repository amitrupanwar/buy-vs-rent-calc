## 2. Information We Collect & Process

### 2.1 Local User Data (Financial Inputs & Saved Profiles)
- **Financial Parameters**: Property prices, loan interest rates, down payments, rent amounts, investment return rates, and saved profile names entered into the calculator.
- **Local On-Device Storage**: All your financial calculations and saved decision profiles are stored **100% locally on your device** using Android's Room Database and SharedPreferences.
- **No Remote Transmission**: Your personal financial parameters and decision profiles are **never transmitted, uploaded, or stored** on any external server or cloud service owned by us.

### 2.2 Advertising & Analytical Data (Google AdMob & Play Services)
Our application integrates **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive banner advertisements. To serve ads and measure ad performance, Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information, including:
- **Advertising Identifiers**: Android Advertising ID (AAID).
- **Device Identifiers & Technical Info**: Device model, operating system version, mobile network information, and IP address.
- **App Usage & Interaction Data**: Ad viewability metrics, click interactions, and app diagnostic/performance logs.

Google AdMob uses this information to deliver, personalize, and measure the effectiveness of advertisements displayed within the app.

## 5. Your Data Control & Privacy Rights

### 5.1 Deleting Local Saved Data
You have complete control over your locally stored financial data. You can delete individual saved decision checks at any time from the **History** tab inside the app, or clear all app data permanently via your Android system settings (`Settings -> Apps -> Buy vs Rent Calc -> Storage -> Clear Data`).

### 5.2 Opting Out of Personalized Advertising
You can reset or opt-out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings:
1. Open your Android **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Google -> Ads** (or **Privacy -> Ads**).
3. Select **"Delete Advertising ID"** or enable **"Opt out of Ads Personalization."**
