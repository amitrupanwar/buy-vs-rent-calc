## 2. Comprehensive Financial Calculation Disclaimer & Risk Notice

### 2.1 Informational Simulation Only (Not Financial Advice)
All outputs generated by the Buy vs Rent Calculator—including but not limited to projected final net worths, monthly EMI amortizations, property value appreciation, opportunity investment portfolio compounding, total cash outflows, tax liability deductions, and break-even crossover points—are **mathematical estimations derived strictly from user-specified input parameters**. 

This application is designed solely as an **educational and comparative decision-support simulation**. It does **NOT** constitute binding financial, investment, legal, mortgage, accounting, or tax advice.

### 2.2 Market Volatility & Regional Variations
Real-world financial markets, real estate values, and economic conditions are volatile and unpredictable. Users should be aware that:
- **Property Price Appreciation**: Housing appreciation rates vary significantly by city, neighborhood, property condition, and macroeconomic shifts. Past or assumed appreciation rates do not guarantee future property values.
- **Rental Escalations & Upkeep**: Rent increases, maintenance expenses, property taxes, and relocation costs fluctuate over time based on local market supply and inflation.
- **Loan Interest Rates & Inflation**: Mortgage interest rates, refinancing terms, and bank lending conditions are subject to central bank policies and economic changes.
- **Tax Laws & Investment Returns**: Mutual fund returns, equity markets, fixed income yields, and capital gains tax slabs vary by jurisdiction and change over time.

### 2.3 User Responsibility
The final financial, real estate, or investment decision rests **solely with the user**. We accept no liability whatsoever for financial losses, investment outcomes, or decisions made based on calculations generated by this application. Users are strongly advised to independently verify all calculations and consult certified financial planners, licensed real estate brokers, and qualified tax advisors before making financial commitments.

## 3. Information We Collect & Process

### 3.1 Local User Data (Financial Inputs & Saved Profiles)
- **Financial Input Parameters**: Property prices, loan interest rates, down payments, rent amounts, investment ROI %, and custom profile titles entered into the calculator.
- **Local On-Device Storage**: All your calculations, input states, and saved decision profiles are stored **100% locally on your device** using Android's Room Database and SharedPreferences.
- **No Remote Server Transmission**: Your personal financial parameters and saved profiles are **never transmitted, uploaded, backed up to our servers, or shared with third parties**.

### 3.2 Advertising & Analytical Data (Google AdMob & Play Services)
Our application integrates **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive banner advertisements to keep the application free. To serve ads and measure performance, Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical information, including:
- **Advertising Identifiers**: Android Advertising ID (AAID).
- **Device Identifiers & Technical Telemetry**: Device model, operating system version, mobile carrier, screen resolution, and IP address.
- **App Usage & Interaction Data**: Ad viewability metrics, ad click interactions, and crash logs.

Google AdMob uses this information in accordance with Google's privacy standards to deliver and measure advertisements.

## 5. Your Data Control & Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

### 5.1 Deleting Local Saved Data
You have complete control over your locally stored financial data. You can delete individual saved decision checks at any time from the **History** tab inside the app, or delete all app data permanently via your Android system settings (`Settings -> Apps -> Buy vs Rent Calc -> Storage -> Clear Data`).

### 5.2 Opting Out of Personalized Advertising
You can reset or opt-out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings:
1. Open your Android **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Google -> Ads** (or **Privacy -> Ads**).
3. Select **"Delete Advertising ID"** or enable **"Opt out of Ads Personalization."**
